Overview

AMIv2 (Aircraft Monitoring & Instrumentation) is the onboard data system for our Chulalongkorn University High Altitude Research Club entry to the Air Cargo Challenge 2026. As Head of Avionics, I designed a custom 2-layer STM32 board that measures power, motion, altitude, and GPS throughout a flight, computes the competition score in real time, and logs everything to an SD card for post-flight analysis.

Hardware

A compact 2-layer PCB designed in KiCad, built around an STM32 microcontroller:

SensorPartMeasures
6-axis IMUBMI160Acceleration & rotation
BarometerBMP280Altitude & temperature
Power monitorINA226Bus voltage & current
GNSSu-blox GPSPosition, speed & distance

Firmware

  • FreeRTOS-based firmware in C++ (STM32CubeIDE + HAL), with each sensor running on its own task.
  • Kalman filtering to smooth noisy sensor signals into clean flight data.
  • A flight-phase state machine (idle → climbing → distance → landing) that adapts logging rates from 1 Hz on the ground to 100 Hz in flight.
  • Real-time competition score computed in-flight from distance, payload, and a current-draw penalty.
  • SD-card logging to CSV, with the board doubling as a USB drive so logs can be pulled off the aircraft directly.
